A trading bot for your phone that shows you the real numbers.

tt runs in Termux on Android. It backtests strategies with real fees, slippage and funding, tests them on data they never saw, paper trades on live prices, and only then trades or copy-trades on Bybit and 100+ other exchanges, with hard loss limits.

No system is guaranteed to make money

  • EU brokers must disclose that roughly 70–80% or more of retail CFD accounts lose money.
  • Of Brazilian day traders who kept going for more than 300 days, 97% lost money (Chague, De-Losso & Giovannetti, 2019).
  • Copy-trading leaderboards show survivors, not the leaders who blew up. tt is built to find out whether an idea works before it touches money.

    What the numbers say

      What it does

      Pure Python standard library at its core, so it installs in seconds on a phone. ccxt is only needed to place real orders.

      Honest backtests

      Fills at the next bar, fees, slippage, gaps through stops and perpetual funding. Monte Carlo reshuffles estimate how bad drawdowns could get.

      Walk-forward testing

      Tunes parameters on the past and judges them only on unseen data, which is the best guard against curve-fitting.

      Paper and demo trading

      Trade live prices with fake money. Bybit demo mode is supported: real order handling, no real funds.

      Risk limits that bite

      Positions are sized so a stop costs 1% of the account. Trading pauses after a daily loss limit, and everything is closed at a maximum drawdown.

      Copy trading

      Mirrors a leader's positions into many follower accounts, each with its own size, leverage cap and kill switch. It corrects itself after missed updates.

      Pre-flight check

      tt check reads live exchange minimums, tells you the account size you need, and flags API keys that can withdraw money.

      Sports prediction markets

      Today's games on Kalshi and Polymarket side by side, a whale scanner, and a stink-bid bot, with a backtest on a season of games. Open the sports board.

      Install on Termux

      Get Termux from F-Droid or its GitHub releases, then:

      # one-time setup
pkg install -y git
git clone https://github.com/mkb08/Tt1 && cd Tt1
bash install_termux.sh --ccxt

# test, then paper trade on live Bybit prices
python -m tt fetch --exchange bybit --symbol BTC/USDT --timeframe 4h --days 1460 --out data/btc.csv
python -m tt walkforward --data data/btc.csv --strategy trend
cp config.bybit-spot.example.json config.json
python -m tt check --config config.json
scripts/run.sh paper --config config.json
      1. Backtest and walk-forward. Drop anything that only works in-sample.
      2. Paper trade for at least a month. Compare the results with the backtest.
      3. Demo trade on Bybit: set "mode": "demo" and use a demo API key.
      4. Go live small, in a sub-account, with money you can afford to lose.

      Trading on Bybit from Kenya

      The essentials, as of October 2026. The full guide has sources and step-by-step setup.

      Licensing deadline: 4 November 2026

      Kenya's VASP Act requires exchanges to be licensed by the Capital Markets Authority. Law firms report 4 November 2026 as the compliance deadline for existing platforms. Nothing public confirms that Bybit has a licence. Keep only trading money on the exchange and have a second exchange ready.

      Tax

      The 3% Digital Asset Tax was repealed in 2025 and replaced by a 10% excise duty on exchange fees. Whether your own profits are taxable is unclear, so keep the bot's trade logs and ask a KRA-registered adviser.

      Start with spot

      • About 15–40 USDT is the minimum for BTC spot. BTC perps need about 200–400 USDT, because the smallest order is 0.001 BTC.
      • Spot can't be liquidated and pays no funding. Shorting didn't improve results in the tests.

      API keys and M-Pesa

      • Never enable withdrawals on a key. Keys without a fixed IP reportedly expire after about 90 days, and mobile data IPs change, so a small server with a fixed IP is the long-term fix.
      • For P2P, release USDT only after the money shows in your M-Pesa app. Fake confirmation SMS messages are the usual scam.

      Backtests use Binance spot price history, which closely tracks Bybit's. Past results don't predict future ones. Nothing here is financial, legal or tax advice. Only trade money you can afford to lose.
