Stink bids on sports: do they catch panicking whales?

The idea, from a 10 October 2026 stream: rest a buy 30% below each team's price on Kalshi and Polymarket, keep it up until halftime, and wait for a whale who has to sell. When one fills, buy the other team on the other venue to lock the gap. This page shows those bids on today's games with live prices, and what happened when the rule was tested on a season of real games.

Run it yourself

The same tools run in Termux on your phone. Paper trading is the default and needs no account.

# today's games, both venues, the cross-venue lock after fees
python -m tt games --leagues nfl,nba --live

# whale scanner in the terminal
python -m tt whales --min-usd 1000

# the stink-bid bot: paper by default
cp stink.example.json stink.json
python -m tt stink --config stink.json --dry-run
scripts/run.sh stink --config stink.json

Prices come from Kalshi, Polymarket and ESPN's public data and can lag by seconds. Nothing here is financial advice. Check whether prediction markets are legal where you live, and only risk money you can afford to lose.
